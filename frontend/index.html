How PulseRead Works

A complete AI pipeline — from raw facial video to clinical-grade heart rate, HRV & stress insights.

01
🎬

Video Pre-Check

Validates duration, brightness, and codec before any processing begins.

Input Validation
→
02
🔲

Face Detection & Multi-ROI

Extracts forehead and cheek ROIs using MediaPipe's 468 landmarks. Live mode can track several faces at once.

MediaPipe · 468 Landmarks
→
03
📡

Dual-Path rPPG Extraction

Runs two parallel paths — downsampled for BPM and full FPS for HRV detail.

Green Channel · Dual FPS
→
04
🧠

FFT + CNN Ensemble

CNN and FFT run in ensemble; confidence threshold picks the best BPM estimate.

Custom CNN · FFT · Ensemble
05
💓

HRV & PRV Computation

Detects IBIs from peaks and computes RMSSD, SDNN, plus a fused FM + AM + baseline breathing rate.

RMSSD · SDNN · Respiration
→
06
📊

Stress & Personal Baseline

Classifies stress (Very Low→High), benchmarks against your own history, and runs baseline → challenge → recovery experiments.

Stress · Baseline · Challenge Mode
→
07
📄

Results, Explainability & Report

Visualises signals and bundles insights into a downloadable PDF report.

PDF Report · Visualisations · AI Insights
